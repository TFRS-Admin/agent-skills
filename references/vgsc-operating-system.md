## Usage

For substantive autonomous work governed by VGSC:

1. Read the canonical dossier rather than relying on a summary.
2. Reconcile its doctrine against the active project's canonical artifacts and local repository instructions.
3. Follow this repository's `AGENTS.md` skill-driven execution rules: audit the complete `skills/` catalog, invoke every applicable skill, and do not bypass an applicable skill with ad-hoc implementation.
4. Treat chat/session narration as transient; validated project artifacts remain the source of truth.
5. If the canonical VGSC source cannot be accessed, record the access gap and do not invent missing doctrine.

## Supervisory integration

The `agents/vgsc-supervisor.md` persona exists to audit long-running autonomous execution against:

- the VGSC dossier,
- canonical project state,
- work-packet acceptance criteria,
- skill applicability/use,
- evidence quality,
- scope/architecture drift,
- consequence-blind corrections,
- and unresolved defect propagation.

The supervisor does not replace the worker or become a router persona. Multi-agent composition remains the responsibility of the orchestrator.
